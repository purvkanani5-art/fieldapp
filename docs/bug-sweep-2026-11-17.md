# Bug sweep, 17 Nov 2026: what a field employee could hit, A to Z

Scope: everything a field employee does, from sign-in to planner tasks assigned to them. Three code reviews, then
each finding was checked against the code. The real ones were fixed and each fix has an automatic check
(`.cache/kl_bugsweep_check.py`, 36 server checks; `.cache/kl-bugsweep-ui.mjs`, 12 phone checks). All the earlier
test suites still pass.

## Signing in and the phone

| # | Bug | What it did | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The sign-in key was kept in a file on the live server's temporary disk | Every time the server woke from sleep, **everyone was silently signed out**; together with bug 2, work saved offline was then deleted | The key is kept in the database, which is backed up and restored |
| 2 | An expired sign-in deleted the phone's saved items | Check-ins, trips and stock entries waiting for signal were thrown away | Sending stops and everything is kept until the person signs in again |
| 3 | Saved items were not tied to a person | On a shared phone, A's offline check-in was sent as B's | Each saved item carries its owner; it is sent only when that person is signed in |
| 4 | A shared phone kept the last person's anonymous complaint open | The next person could read (and reply to) an anonymous or POSH thread | Cleared at every sign-in and sign-out |
| 5 | The starting PIN from HR never had to be changed | Many people would keep 1234 forever | First sign-in shows "Set your own PIN"; the server refuses any save until it is changed |
| 6 | A PIN reset did not sign the old phone out | A lost phone stayed signed in for up to 30 days | A PIN reset signs out every phone of that person |

## Attendance (check-in and check-out)

| # | Bug | What it did | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | A check-out saved offline arrived after the nightly "missed check-out" close | The real check-out (selfie, GPS, time) was thrown away and the day showed as missed | Saved items are sent before anything else when the app opens; a late real check-out replaces the automatic one |

## Looked at, not changed

- **Time zone on the live server**: already set to India time, so times, "late" and the nightly close are right.
- **Wrong-PIN lockout** (5 tries, 15 minutes) resets if the server restarts. Low risk.
- **Survey answers** could, with direct database access, be matched to people by order. Only someone with
  database access could do this; can be hardened later.
- **Features, not bugs, for later**: half-day leave; withdrawing a pending request; a yearly allowance for leave types
  added in Settings → Lists (until HR sets one in HR settings, no balance is checked for that type).
